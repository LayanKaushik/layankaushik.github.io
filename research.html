"Research on Spatial Distribution of Moisture Content in Cement Mortar Structures"
IASc-INSA-NASI Summer Research Fellowship, 2018
Developed novel radial basis function methods to predict moisture content in cement structures, critical for durability assessment. Implemented Gaussian and inverse multiquadric interpolation techniques to estimate moisture levels where experimental data was unavailable, with theoretical validation. Built using Python scientific computing stack.
