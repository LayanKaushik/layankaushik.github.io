Layan Kaushik

Research interests in Applied Econometrics, Machine Learning, and Applied Microeconomics

I'm an Economics Researcher at Amazon, where I apply econometrics and machine learning to solve complex business problems at scale. My work focuses on developing statistical methods for causal inference and experimentation.

At Amazon, I collaborate closely with Professor Alexandre Mas and Principal Economist Mingyu Chen on survey methodology, causal inference, and experimentation. I develop machine learning approaches to tackle identification challenges and non-response bias in large-scale observational data.

I hold an MS in Economics and Computation from Duke University and a Bachelor of Technology in Civil Engineering from IIT Roorkee. My engineering background instilled a systemic approach to problem-solving that shapes how I tackle complex economic questions. My research combines rigorous economic theory with computational methods. I'm particularly interested in developing statistical tools that make causal inference practical and scalable by accounting for the interconnected nature of real-world systems.

Research Focus: Applied econometrics, causal inference, machine learning, and computational economics.

Technical Expertise: Advanced econometrics methods, statistical package development, machine learning applications in economics, and computational statistics.
